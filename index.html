01 — About WaterLife

A clean ocean is a public health issue, an economic issue, and a moral one.

Founded on Victoria Island, WaterLife works where Lagos's lagoon system meets the Atlantic — one of the busiest and most ecologically sensitive coastlines in West Africa. We monitor water quality, organise shoreline clean-ups, restore mangrove nurseries, and train the next generation of marine stewards.

We believe cleanliness and purity of marine life aren't abstract ideals — they're measurable conditions we track tide by tide, and defend community by community.

"You cannot separate the health of the water from the health of the people who live beside it."

The blue economy is our long game.

A healthy ocean is Lagos's least-used asset. We work across four pillars to turn conservation into sustainable income for coastal communities.

Pillar 01

Sustainable Fisheries

Working with fishing communities on catch limits, gear standards, and access to markets that reward sustainable practice.

Pillar 02

Coastal Tourism

Helping waterfront communities build eco-tourism income around clean beaches and healthy mangrove ecosystems.

Pillar 03

Clean Shipping & Ports

Advocating for waste management standards at ports and terminals along the Lagos harbour approach.

Pillar 04

Green Livelihoods

Training in mangrove nursery management, water testing, and craft trades built around ocean materials.

Our work on the water.

Four programmes run year-round from our Victoria Island base, each measured against clear, published targets.

Shoreline & Lagoon Clean-ups

Monthly clean-up operations along Victoria Island, Bar Beach, and connecting lagoon settlements, logged and published as public data.

Since 2021

Water Quality Monitoring

Weekly testing of turbidity, salinity, and pollutant levels at twelve fixed points across the lagoon and open coast.

12 monitoring points

Mangrove Restoration

Nursery-grown mangrove seedlings replanted along degraded creek edges to rebuild natural coastal defences.

3 active nurseries

Community Education & Advocacy

School and community workshops on marine health, plus policy engagement with local and state authorities.

40+ workshops a year
